# Why

I am more passionate about decentralized systems then any other topic, I have been leanring and publishing and workign in the field for 8 years now. I wish dearly that others could see the beauty in these self-healing scalable systems that lack a single point of failure, these systems that mirror the behaviors independently developed in nature (nature is heavily decentralized). I saw the possiblilty at NASA as they build an interplanatry network, at Anduril where they are building reseilient drone swarms, and all around us in the bees and ants and plants. Due to the scalable nature of these protocols, we see these behaviors range from the micro level in nano-particles, to cars in cities, and even up to the interplanatary systems being built for the future.

This field is ideal for visual exploration, it deals with physical phenomanom, is made up of simple parts we can zoom into and fully understand, we can zoom out and see ergent behavior, is used in systems we see in our daily life, and just happens to produce satisfying animations such as propogating waves and self-organization.

These systems are becoming more relvent today with drones taking over the battlefield, self driving cars slowly being deployed, wildfires increasing in frequency that require robots to extinguish, increasing exploration of the seas depths, 

My blog posts take a long time (1-2 months) to create as I do them in my freeitme, so in order to create a full chapter and eventually a full book, I will be taking some time off to focus on creating this. If I am able to help people to see the exciting oppertunity in this field and its ideas, I beleive it is one of the more important things I can do, as I beleive much of our future will focus on more decentralized and robust robot systems.
