## Chapters

Each section begins with an intro about the topic being covered, listing what the topic is, what it applies to with an example or two, and what about the topic we will learn.

We should consider in every chapter how well a solution scales, if it is robust to movement, failures, scaling, noise, and see where it fails. We may want a repeating header for this portion of a section. We can refernce real systems as interesting sources of failures of these systems. Ant death spirals, cancer, etc..
